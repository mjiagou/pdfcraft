## Why Are PDF Files So Huge in the First Place?

Understanding what bloats your PDF helps you pick the right optimization:
1. **Uncompressed High-DPI Scans**: Mobile scanning apps and office photocopiers frequently embed raw raster images at 300 to 600 DPI, making each page take 5MB to 10MB.
2. **Full Font Embedding**: Many documents embed complete font families (especially Asian font sets that are 15MB+), even if only a few characters are used.
3. **Redundant Metadata & Revisions**: Incremental saves in software like Photoshop or Acrobat preserve historical edit history and invisible streams.

A proper compression engine targets oversized images and redundant streams without sacrificing text sharpness or vector fidelity.
